# World 2, level 3. Teach: walking off a ledge is a shorter flight than jumping.
# Target first-time solve: 30-60 s (a jump off the edge after w2-01/w2-02, a drop through the red floor, then
#   the same sprint without the jump).
# Layout (one screen, 30 x 13): left wall, safe ledge (cols 1-10, spawn against the wall, open sky: stop and
#   think) -> a 3-tile pit at the foot of the ledge (cols 11-13, v2) -> 11-tile red floor (cols 14-24) a drop
#   of exactly 4 tiles below the ledge, over the same pit -> safe exit pad (cols 25-29, exit 2 tiles tall at col 27).
# Version 2 (design-lead B6-06): the pit at the foot of the ledge shows a gap, which invites the world-2 jump.
# Sprint (gear 4, red at x = 4.73) and walk off the edge: 29 airborne steps, landing 5.8 tiles out (x = 17.1),
#   under the 6-tile drag point, so you land red on the red floor. Jump off the edge (or a coyote jump just
#   after it) and you fly 10.65+ tiles, drag to yellow at 6, and fall through the red floor.
# The drop must stay exactly 4 tiles: from 5 tiles the walk-off lands yellow too (0 of 1,120 lines win).
# The exit pad starts at col 25 so that no jump off the ledge (coyote jumps included) reaches it.
# No roof (world 2). No hint glyph (none in the plan).
