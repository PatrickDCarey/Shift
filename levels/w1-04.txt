# World 1, level 4. Teach: slowing down takes distance too.
# Target first-time solve: 45-90 s.
# Layout (35 x 13, scrolls): safe runway (cols 0-6, 5.9 tiles from the spawn) -> red bridge (cols 7-12)
#   under a 2-tile safe roof -> 6-tile safe pad (cols 13-18, open sky: stop and think) -> 12-tile blue
#   bridge (cols 19-30, no roof) -> exit pad.
# Sprint across the red bridge, then be blue by the blue bridge: from sprint, blue comes 3.24 tiles
# after you shift down (or let go). Too early and you turn yellow on the red bridge and fall; too late and
# you are still green on the blue bridge and fall. Either shift down three times while running (window
# 27 steps, x 11.3-16.5) or let go on the pad, stop, shift down to gear 1 and walk on (24 steps).
# The blue bridge has no roof on purpose: a jump into a roof face would wall-stop you in the air and drop
# you to gear 1 instantly (skipping the brake). Instead it is 12 tiles long, so no takeoff from the pad
# clears it, and any non-blue landing on it falls through.
