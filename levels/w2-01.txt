#   (cols 4-10, 7 tiles) over a pit -> 4-tile gap (cols 11-14) -> green landing pad (cols 15-16) -> safe exit

#   jump flies 4.8 tiles and lands green. Jump window: takeoff at x 9.81-11.81 (21 steps, about 0.35 s).
#   Jump early and you land in the gap: left, right, shift and jump do nothing in the air.
