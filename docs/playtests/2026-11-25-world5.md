## Notes
w5-01: bolts passed harmlessly through me as blue, I learned the rule by walking under them; killed when I shifted to red early. Solved by walking blue under both bolts, then E x3 after column 2 and crossing the red floor. Shifts are fast up; downshift is NOT instant, speed decays over distance (see w5-02). w5-02 in progress.

w5-02: white bolts did make sense (standing still = white = hit; moving as any colour is safe, so "keep moving"). The real cost was gear changes between floors: upshift is instant, downshift needs a few frames of coasting, and the middle platform is short, so I fell 4 times (3 as a downshift that was too late/early, 1 too early on red). Easy to understand each death, fiddly to execute; my frame-timed plans were not repeatable run to run, so I played it in chunks.

w5-03 (in progress): the grey bolts are small pale diamonds with very little contrast against the dark background and read as sparkles, not as danger; I only understood they kill (any colour, green and yellow included) after dying. Jumping over the first bolt on the green bridge worked. Last stretch (step down to the low floor, launcher hanging above the door end) cost me 3 deaths: I jumped into bolts in mid-air because the bolts travel at head height on the low floor and my jump arc meets them. Gear drag in the air also drops me from yellow to green mid-jump, which is surprising.

w5-03 result: cleared on the 7th death. Final solution: green across the bridge with one jump over the first bolt, jump onto the middle platform, E to yellow across the yellow floor, then off the step with ONE jump over the first low bolt and plain walking after that (on the low floor the bolts pass over my head; jumping there is what killed me). Nothing told me that; I inferred it from three deaths. "Go right after a bolt ends" did not occur to me as such; I treated it as jump-timing. 10-death rule not reached.

w5-04: the arrow plus shrinking bar clearly told me the front was behind me and how close (bar shortens as it nears); the white wall appearing at the left edge confirmed it. Gear 1 (blue) is too slow to outrun it at the start, so I used green past the first two lanes (red and yellow bolts both miss green), then red over the red floor (red also passes the yellow bolt), then green through the four alternating lanes. Lane colours matched the bolt colours, which made "which gear" readable (the launcher icon and floor-marker colour). Both deaths: the tiny blue floor strip after the small platform; I jumped too early, then did the downshift on the 1.5-tile platform, which worked. Front caught up to the door almost exactly, so it felt tight and good.

w5-05 (in progress): room 1 is w5-01 again (red bolt then red floor); walking green past the bolt then E to red works and is faster than blue. Room 2: red floor, gap with a yellow bolt column, yellow floor lower down, then a platform and a 7-tile blue floor. The red-to-yellow-over-a-gap hop took 3 deaths (jump a few frames early, shift in mid-air). The long blue floor needs the downshift done on the platform before it (I did Q twice right after landing); gear 1 is painfully slow to cross 7 tiles, but I understood every death. The checkpoint post is a small flag on a pole, visible from far away on a long straight, easy to miss at a glance but I saw it for ~200 frames before reaching it.

## w5-05 route as played
Room 1 (red bolt, red floor), room 2 (red floor, yellow bolt gap, yellow floor), long blue floor, post 1 (small flag on a pole, white flag once passed), vertical moving slab with spikes underneath carrying me up beside a launcher firing a line of grey bolts at head height (alive, they never killed me there, unclear why), a long wall top, a green moving bridge, a drop from the wall top onto a red floor, post 2, then a hurdles-style finale (green bridge with a low bolt from the right launcher, jump, yellow floor, door). Post 3 and the end were NOT reached.

## Findings
- Checkpoints: respawn-on-death goes to the last post (felt fair and quick), but R (restart) goes all the way to the level start, which surprised me once and wasted a replay. I noticed both posts well before reaching them (small, but on long flat runs).
- Possibly broken (not confirmed): at the wall edge before the red floor, pressing E twice 5-7 frames apart while stepping off the edge only registered once (gear 3 yellow, then fell through the red floor), twice in a row. Same spacing works fine on the ground. Repro: run green along the wall top, tap E, E at the edge, fall onto the red floor. Fix on my side was to shift well before the edge.
- Camera look-ahead: I could see the next floor colour and hazards early on almost every stretch (the post and the next obstacle appear ~200 px ahead), which helped; I never got a blind drop.
- Gear 1 (blue) is very slow; crossing the 7-tile blue floor and outrunning the chase front in w5-04 feels sluggish.
- Fun: w5-04 and w5-01 were best; w5-02 and w5-03 were fiddly because gear changes have to be timed to platform lengths by trial and error.

## Top five changes
1. Make the low grey bolts in w5-03 and w5-05 clearly dangerous (bigger, brighter, coloured trail); they read as sparkles.
2. Let gear shifts register in the air (or show a cooldown cue); the failed second E cost me several deaths.
3. Make R restart from the last post, or label "R: restart level" clearly when a post is active.
4. Lengthen the middle platforms in w5-02 and w5-05 slightly, or show how long a downshift takes.
5. Speed up gear 1 a little so slow sections do not feel like waiting.
