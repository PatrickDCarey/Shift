## Answers to design's ten questions
1. **w2-04 failed jumps.** About 6 failed tries before the README, 3 more after, 1 win. Most failures were my own error (I shifted up twice standing, so I was already yellow and fell on the green floor). From the screen alone I could not tell why each failed: the player just drops through the green floor or into the pit and respawns. The idea (shift up on the floor, jump within a fraction of a second) is not guessable; the level has no hint glyph. A voter will mash. The window is 0.2 s.
2. **Kerb (w3-02 to w3-05).** In w3-02, yes, within the first run: I held right, the top body walked into a 1-tile grey block and stopped, and its HUD row turned from blue to white while the lower body kept going. That reads as "a block holds one body still". Clicked at first sight (about 6 s in). What I could not tell cold is that holding right keeps it pinned and a green jump from a back-up clears it. I did not see a kerb in w3-03 (a hanging block instead); w3-04/05 I did not get far enough.
3. **Stuck in the pocket (w3-02), found R?** Nothing on screen told me. I dropped the runner into the closed pocket under the green bridge and waited 12 s: no text, no marker, no auto-reset, runner white against the wall, twin parked at its exit (`b6-06`). I only pressed R because I had read the controls screen. A voter who has not opened Controls will sit there or quit. (I did not reach the w3-03 pocket.)
4. **Blue strip (w3-04/w3-05).** I read it as "a short coloured floor in the middle of safe ground that you must be blue to cross", the same grammar as every bridge. It is readable as a thing to act on only in the sense that the runner is blue anyway after the mid-air block hit. I could not work out why it is there (what it asks of me) and did not finish either level, so I cannot say if it earns its place.
5. **w2-02 wipe in the air.** Yes, noticed: the screen flashes brown and a white arc trails the runner at the moment red turns yellow mid-jump (`b6-04`); HUD shows a small drag glyph under the slot. I connected it to "something changed my colour in the air", and the colour change from red to yellow I read as slowing down. I did not connect it to "air drag" by name.
6. **w2-03 stuck?** Not at all. 1 attempt, 4 s. Shift up to red, run, walk off the ledge, land on the red bridge. It is w1-03 with a drop. It teaches nothing new (no jump, no decision). Not hard, but no "rebuilt" feel.
7. **Hints.** Shown in w1-01 (arrow keys), w1-02 (E/X up, Q/Z down glyphs placed over the platforms), w2-01 (E and Space), w3-01 (move + shift glyphs). All were read and helped; none was wrong or in the way. w1-02's glyphs are well placed. The one place I wanted one: w1-03 (nothing says "back up for a run-up") and w2-04 (nothing about the jump timing). Hints stop in w2-02, which is where the first unguided decision starts, fine.
8. **Pause / results / controls.** Results screen is clear: time, best, FIRST CLEAR, ENTER next, R retry, ESC levels; ENTER worked. Controls screen is accurate: every key I tried matched the screen (E, Q, Space, R, Enter, Esc). Controls was reached by Down, Down, Enter from the title; "SHOW STICKY KEYS TIP" sits right above BACK and could be hit by mistake but is harmless. I did not exercise the pause menu this sitting (see "Not verified").
9. **Why you fell, and the exit arrow.** Fall cue: yes. When I dropped through a bridge in the wrong colour, a small checker tile (the gear pattern needed) appeared at the bridge's start, and the screen tints maroon for a moment (seen in w1-03 and w1-04). I understood "that bridge wanted that pattern" because the patterns match the HUD slots. Exit arrow: yes, a door with a right-pointing arrow shows at the screen edge when the exit is off-screen (w1-04, w3-03, w3-04, w3-05). Immediately obvious.
10. **Title to moving.** About 3 s: title shows PLAY highlighted with "1-1 FIRST STEPS" beside it, Enter starts. There is a Sticky Keys tip card at the bottom of w1-01 (Enter to dismiss) but it did not block play. I did not hit a moment of not knowing what to press in w1-01 (arrow-key glyphs float over the player). The first moment of doubt is w1-03.

## Cheese
None found. w3-03 solved with the jump only (no shifts at all after the first three), which README says is the intent.

## Things that looked broken (for QA)
- **No broken behaviour that stopped play.** One for QA to confirm: in w3-02, if the runner drops into the pocket, nothing resets and nothing prompts (design chose R; see requests). Repro: `?level=w3-02`, hold Right 4.8 s, tap E, hold Right 4.5 s, release, tap Q, hold Left 0.9 s, release, hold Right 0.75 s then Space: runner lands in the pocket under the green bridge; wait 12 s, no cue.
- Rapid presses in the same frame: shifting up (or down) three or four times within one frame behaves like one press, or the gear lags behind. It looked like presses were coalesced; not a bug for a human, but it is a trap for a script and for anyone with a hardware macro.

## Top five changes
1. **Give the pocket (w3-02, w3-03) a cue**: when a body is in the closed pocket, show "R: restart" or auto-reset after about 2 s. Right now a player who never opened Controls is stuck.
2. **w3-03/w3-04/w3-05: teach the hanging block.** Nothing says "jump into the block to lose gear". A single hint glyph (jump) near the red runway, as in w2-01, would do. I only got the mechanism from the README.
3. **w2-04: soften or hint.** 0.2 s window plus no hint plus no way to see how close you were is the highest voter-quit risk in the build. Widen the window or show a mid-air hint at the green floor.
4. **w2-03: make it matter or cut it.** It is a 4 s walk; the rebuild did not add a decision. Fold in a tiny second decision or merge with w2-02.
5. **w1-03: a hint toward the left wall** ("back up for speed" glyph, or start the player further right with the wall visible). Four attempts before I got that distance is the first drop-off point.

## Scores (as a jam voter; sound not heard)
- **Overall 3.5**: a clear idea with a good teaching ramp in worlds 1 and 3, but several levels need a spoiler to learn.
- **Gameplay 3.5**: the Tandem idea is the best thing in it; timing windows in w1-04 and w2-04 are tight for what they teach.
- **Graphics 4**: patterns + colour + HUD gear rows read well; the fall cue and the exit arrow are nice touches; the air wipe is a highlight.
- **Audio n/a**: headless, could not judge.
- **Innovation 4**: one pair of keys driving two bodies on a speed-colour mechanic is unusual.
- **Theme 4**: "shift" is the verb, the colour, and the key; it lands.

Single change that would most improve the game: tell the player when they are stuck (pocket with no cue) and what the hanging block is for; without those, world 3 reads as "I do not know what I did wrong".
