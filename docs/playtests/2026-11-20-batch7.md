## w3-04 Clamp (cold, finished) and w3-05 Redshift (cold, not finished)

### w3-04 Clamp
- **Understood the new idea?** After many tries, and only partly. Rules I worked out from the screen: shift-up is almost instant (three X/E taps in the first second turn both bodies red); the twin starts only 7 tiles from the wall yet faces a red bridge, so you must be red before it; a jump from the twin's platform edge makes the runner hit the hanging block, which drops the runner to white then blue (it lands on the blue strip) while the twin, in the air, drifts from red to yellow and lands on the yellow bridge. After that the two speeds diverge (twin fast, runner slow). My final read: "gear is shared, speed is per body, the block is a gear brake for one body". I did not read this from a hint; I got it by experiment.
- **Attempts / time:** about 16 scripted tries (6 of them wasted on the key slip and standing shifts, about 5 exploring the red bridge, 5 finding the finish). Clear at 7.8 s of game time. Solution I found: three shift-ups right at the start, jump at the twin platform edge (block hit), hold right, then three more shift-ups about 2.8 s later when the twin is on the grey slab (both bodies red; red bridges are then solid), no more jumps.
- **Confusion:** (1) At the start both bodies stand at the left wall, the twin on a 7-tile slab ending in a red bridge. Nothing says that shift-ups are instant, so a cold player walks off the slab at blue speed and falls: that is where I died about 8 times (`b7-03` shows the working opening). (2) After the opening the runner and twin separate by 15 tiles; the camera tracks the twin and the runner is partly off screen; the twin's exit arrow (green) appears at the edge. (3) The runner's red bridge on the top lane arrives about when the twin jumps; a shared jump put the runner on the bridge at blue speed twice (it died white at the bridge).
- **Difficulty 4**, fair once you know "shift up before the red bridge". The first move (the jump into the block) is not guessable; w3-03 has a SPACE glyph near the block, w3-04 has none, which would be fine if w3-03 had taught it. It did here, but only because I played w3-03 knowing it.
- **Fun 3.5.** The opening jump (runner hits the block, twin keeps its speed) is the best moment in world 3 once it clicks.
- **Cheese:** none found. (Final shift-ups work on the grey slab rather than by jumping the red bridges; the intended route may differ.)
- **Needed "The idea:" line?** Yes, I wanted it after about 10 tries and could not read it; I finished without it, at about 16.

### w3-05 Redshift
- **Understood?** Partly. Same opening as w3-04 works (twin lands on the 4-tile yellow bridge). Then the twin's lane has a blue strip after the grey: I found that tapping shift-down three times on the grey brings both bodies to blue and the twin crosses it. Both lanes then end in a one-tile kerb followed by a short gap, then a yellow bridge in each lane, and the exit in the top lane.
- **Attempts:** about 14 scripted tries. **Not finished.** I cannot get both bodies over the kerb at yellow speed: the twin (ahead of the runner) arrives and stands pinned, white, at the kerb; when I then shift up and jump, the pinned twin comes down short of the gap or both jump too early (`b7-04`). A back-up-and-run-up attempt also failed (timing); I ran out of budget, not out of ideas.
- **Confusion:** the twin waiting white at the kerb while the runner is still walking. Nothing tells me whether to back up for a run or whether a jump from standing is meant to work. It is also the first level where the same keys must serve a blue strip, a kerb and a yellow bridge in one run; the whole thing is about 20 s of play with no checkpoint, so each failed try costs about 20 s.
- **Difficulty:** 4 to 5 for a cold player. Fair in rules, harsh in length, and I cannot say whether the last section is fair because I did not solve it.
- **Needed "The idea:" line?** Yes: after 10 failed tries I wanted it and could not read it, so w3-05 is a not-finished.
- **Cheese:** none found.

## Replays and new features

### Restart card (w3-02)
Repro used: hold Right 4.8 s, tap shift-up, hold Right 4.5 s, release, shift-down, Left 0.9 s, Right 0.75 s then Space. Runner drops into the pocket under the green bridge. About 1 s later a small framed "[R] RESTART" card appears bottom centre (`b7-01`) and stays; pressing R restarted the level. **Verdict: works and clear.** The card is small and sits on the lower lane's slab, so it reads like a tile label; a voter who is looking at the top lane may take a second to see it. A bigger box or a colour change at the bottom of the screen would help. In w3-03 I never got a body into the pocket, so the w3-03 card is **not verified**.

### Hints (onboarding-hints check 12)
- **w1-03:** a shift-up key glyph (R-shift, E, X) floats at the left, over the wall (`b7-05` is w3-03; w1-03 seen in a first frame). It tells me shift-up exists, and because it is placed at the left, it nudges toward the left wall. It did not tell me "back up for a run-up", which was my batch 6 gap, but it is placed well enough to prompt "I should go left first". Not too early (shown from frame 1), not in the way. Partially fixes the gap.
- **w2-04:** shift-up glyph pair plus SPACE / W / up-arrow glyph (`b7-06`), above the green floor. It makes it clear that you need both keys; it does not say "shift first, jump within 0.2 s" or when. It changed what I would try (I would not press only one) but not the timing problem. Verdict: helps, not enough.
- **w3-03:** SPACE / W / up-arrow glyph to the left of the hanging block (`b7-05`). I read it as "jump here", and jumping there is the solution; it is the right placement. It does not say why. It is above the lane, not over the player, so it is easy to miss on the first run but I did see it. Verdict: good, kept me from the batch 6 trap of shifting down in the air.

### Settings screen
Reached from the title (Down x3, Enter). Rows: SOUND ON/OFF, REDUCED MOTION, REDUCED EFFECTS, BACK, with a square filled when on (`b7-02`). Enter toggles each; the cursor row is boxed. It reads clearly. With REDUCED EFFECTS on, the red-brown fall tint and the dither/post effects were gone in w1-01 (the checker cue tile stayed); reduced motion I did not verify visibly. I did not test that the setting survives a reload.

## Top five changes
1. **Teach "shift-ups are instant, jump into the block" before w3-04** (a SPACE glyph at the block in w3-04 as in w3-03, plus one shift glyph at the start). I lost most of my 16 tries to not knowing that the red bridge is crossable.
2. **Show both bodies, or an off-screen marker for the runner, when they separate** (w3-04/05): the camera follows the twin and the runner is out of sight while it matters.
3. **w3-05: shorten the retry** (checkpoint after the twin lands on the yellow bridge, or an easier kerb) and signal what to do about the pinned white twin at the kerb.
4. **Restart card: make it bigger or add a border colour**; it is the first thing a stuck player needs.
5. **w2-04 hint: add timing** (a ring or a small arrow from the shift glyph to the jump glyph).
