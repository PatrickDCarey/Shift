# Playtest, Batch 8 wave 4 (B8-11): hint check on w1-03, w2-04, w3-03, w3-04, w3-05

Build: packaged zip `redshift-qa.zip` (sha256 starts 657135ba), unzipped and served on port 5191, headless Chromium 960x540, real key presses through Playwright. Fresh eyes: I read no level, design or earlier playtest files before playing.

Method caveat, important for every number below: my key presses come from a script, not hands. Each press has about +/-100 ms of jitter and a screenshot costs 100-300 ms, so a "try" for me is a little worse than one for a human who watches the screen. Level timers count from the last restart (R) and keep running across falls, so the "clear time" I quote is the timer of the clearing life where I trust it, otherwise my own estimate of that life.

Evidence: `docs/playtests/evidence/b8-*.png`.

## What I worked out about the rules (so the hint judgments make sense)

- Color = your current speed band. Standing still is white, and white is not solid on any colored tile. Stopping on a colored bridge drops you through it.
- E/Q (and X/Z) change gear. Speed ramps over distance: from a standing start about 300 px to reach gear 4. Gear 3 to gear 4 takes about 35 px.
- You can shift in the air (w2-04 depends on it). I only found this by experiment; nothing in w1-w2 says it.
- In the two-lane levels each runner has its own gear. E/Q add and subtract one from each runner's own gear. The HUD strips do show this (the outlined square differs per lane) but I only understood it in w3-04.
- Jump is a short hop (about 100 px high, about 0.45 s). A jump from standstill goes straight up and down.

## Per level

### w1-03 Runway: cleared on try 2 (1 fall)
- Tries: 2. Clear: about 6 s of actual play (timer read 6:43 because the level had been open while I read screenshots).
- Hint: shift-up glyph (E/X) at the left of the spawn, behind the player (`b8-w1-03-spawn.png`). Seen: immediately. Understood: yes, as "go back there and shift up". Timing: on time and well placed. It sat behind me and the left wall made "back up" the obvious reading.
- Design's check (cleared within 3 falls): **pass.** My one fall was because I backed up only about 130 px, not to the wall. Second try, back to the wall, three E presses, run: cleared (`b8-w1-03-run.png`).
- Confusion: the glyph says "shift up", not "you need distance". A player who walks right and presses E at the glyph's position would fail repeatedly. I would still say the placement does the teaching.
- Difficulty 2/5, fair. Fun 3/5.
- Cheese: none.

### w2-04 Two Colors: cleared on try about 10 (9 falls)
- Tries: about 10 (3 spent on the harness and respawn quirks, see "Broken"). Clear: 0:05.33 on the clearing life.
- Hints: E/X glyph and SPACE glyph stacked above the middle of the green bridge (`b8-w2-04-spawn.png`). Seen: yes, from spawn. Understood: partly. I read them as "shift up, then jump". Timing: roughly on time for the jump (take-off point is about 100-150 px right of the glyph).
- What I thought the rule was, and what it really is: I first assumed gear 3 had to be set on the ground. Real solution: gear 2 across green (one E), a second E just before take-off (while still on green), jump from about 65 px before the green's end, and a further E in the air so the yellow bridge is solid when you land. Pressing E only in the air, or only on the ground, both fail (`b8-w2-04-fail-short.png` shows the typical fall past the green's end at gear 2).
- If you fail it more than 5 times, what would have helped (timing): the take-off window is about 150 ms wide (jump earlier and you land back on green at gear 2 and walk off; later and you fall past the end). Concretely: (1) a second small E glyph over the yellow bridge's start, to say "shift in the air"; (2) a visible take-off marker (a post or tile mark) on the green near its end; (3) show gear-up arrows or the "<" drag glyph near the jump glyph; (4) a few frames of coyote time at the end of the green; (5) make the yellow bridge start 30-40 px earlier so the landing target is bigger. Item (1) is what I wanted most. I learned that air shifts exist only by pressing E mid-flight in an unrelated test.
- Difficulty 4/5 for a first-time player (it needs two ideas at once: shift in the air and a 150 ms window). Fair after the idea clicks, unfair before. Fun 3/5. Solved moment (`b8-w2-04-solved-air-shift.png`) is satisfying.
- Cheese: none found.

### w3-03 Gear Split: cleared on try 5
- Tries: 5. Clear: 0:11.60.
- Hints: new E/X glyph at the spawn (above the top runner), SPACE/W/Up glyph at x about 400 in world space (`b8-w3-03-spawn.png`). Seen: both, immediately. Understood: yes. "Shift up first, jump at the glyph" was the plan I tried first and it was correct. Timing: E glyph on time (needed at once); jump glyph on time (the bottom runner takes off just before the red bridge's end, which is about where the glyph is).
- Not hinted, and I would not have solved it without experimenting: (a) the top runner jumping under the hanging pillar loses all speed, which is how the lanes get different gears. I found it because the top runner went white and slowed after my first jump. It is a nice trick. (b) After the bottom runner reaches its exit I have to press Q three times for the top runner to cross the blue bridge at gear 1. Pressing Q too early (tries 3 and 4) kills the bottom runner on the yellow bridge. The home glyph in the bottom strip tells you when the bottom runner is done, but I only noticed afterwards.
- Restart card: seen. After the top runner fell through the blue bridge into the pocket, a "R RESTART" card appeared at bottom centre, large with a white outline (`b8-w3-03-restart-card-pocket.png`). It is clearly readable at 960x540 and I understood it at once. Two notes: it sits over the bottom lane (covers part of the yellow bridge and the bottom runner's exit approach) and it only shows once I am stuck for a few seconds. I did not time that delay exactly (about 2-3 s).
- Difficulty 3/5. Fair. Fun 4/5, it is the first two-lane level that feels like a puzzle.
- Cheese: none.

### w3-04 Clamp: cleared on try about 8
- Tries: about 8. Clear: 0:08.26.
- "New hints": same E/X glyph at the spawn and SPACE glyph at x about 400 as w3-03 (`b8-w3-04-spawn.png`). Seen and understood, on time, for the first jump. They are not much help for the part that is new: after the jump and the pillar bonk, the top runner (gear 1, crossing the blue bridge) and the bottom runner (gear 3, crossing the yellow bridge) each need to reach gear 4 for their own red bridge. The bottom runner has a long platform, the top runner has only about 190 px.
- What I got wrong, in order: pressed E twice right after the jump (bottom runner at gear 5 on yellow, fell); pressed E once on the platform (bottom ok, top only reached gear 2 and fell through its red bridge, `b8-w3-04-lanes-diverge-gear.png`); finally pressed E three times at the instant the top runner left the blue bridge. That cleared it (`b8-w3-04-solved-both-red.png`). The lesson is that each runner adds the presses to its own gear. I had no hint for this phase at all. A third hint would be useful here: three E marks, or "E E E" text, near the blue bridge's end.
- Difficulty 4/5. Fair once understood, opaque before. Fun 3/5 (the solving moment is good, the failing is long).
- Cheese: none.

### w3-05 Redshift: NOT cleared (about 14 lives, about 10 of them real attempts; roughly 30-40 minutes of wall time)
- Hints seen: SPACE at x about 400 near the spawn (on time, as in w3-03/04), then two glyphs near the kerb: E/X and SPACE/W/Up above the top lane, about 275 px and 115 px before the kerb (`b8-w3-05-kerb-hints-E-and-space.png`). I only saw them when my runners had arrived at the kerb. Understood: E glyph = shift up, SPACE glyph = jump. Early/late/on time: on time for the jump over the kerb as designed, but they are only on screen when you are already there, and nothing says "you need to back up and run at this". I did understand it as "run, shift, jump" but not the exact distances.
- What worked: the two opening jumps (jump 1 at the red bridge's end, jump 2 over the 95 px blue gap in the bottom lane, at about +1.3 s after jump 1). With the same inputs it succeeds about half the time. I reached the kerb with both runners (`b8-w3-05-after-two-jumps-kerb.png`, `b8-w3-05-both-blocked-at-kerb.png`) at least four times.
- Where I am stuck, exactly:
  1. At the kerb the two runners are not at the same distance from their kerb blocks: the top runner is much slower (gear 1) and arrives later. Both lanes' kerbs are at the same x, so I can only jump once for both.
  2. When I back up for a run-up the runners go back by different amounts because their gears differ (bottom 2, top 1): one hold of Left gave about 120 px for the bottom and about 45 px for the top (`b8-w3-05-backed-up-unequal.png`). That means a jump from the same key press is 75 px off for one of them.
  3. I worked out that Q three times first clamps both gears to 1 and makes the backing-up equal (the first test showed equal movement of about 160 px each). I never got to test the full chain (hold right until both are at the kerb, Q Q Q, hold Left 1.5 s, E E, run, jump) because the opening two jumps failed on every attempt after that.
  4. Limits: the bottom lane has a blue (gear 1) bridge about 320 px behind the kerb. Backing up too far, or standing still on it, drops that runner (white is not solid on blue), which resets the whole level and the two opening jumps.
- So I cannot say whether the level is solvable by a normal player from the information given. I can say the success rate of the setup (two precise jumps, then a gear equalising trick, then a run-up of about 250 px, then one more jump) is far below what a jam voter will put up with. A voter would quit here, or already at w3-04.
- Difficulty 5/5. Fair: no, because the equalise-then-run-up step is not hinted anywhere and any failure restarts both opening jumps. Fun 2/5 as it stands.
- Cheese: none found.

## Top five changes

1. Teach per-runner gear explicitly in w3-01 or w3-03 (a one-line caption such as "E and Q change each runner's own gear", or a brief highlight on both HUD strips). It is the key rule for w3-03, w3-04 and w3-05 and I found it by accident in w3-04.
2. Give w3-04 and w3-05 a mid-level checkpoint, or at least a shorter reset, after the opening jumps. w3-05 needs two precise jumps every time before you can even attempt the part that is new.
3. w3-05's kerb: put the run-up room and a back-up hint in the layout (for example a glyph behind the player, as w1-03 does well), make both lanes' distances to their kerbs equal by design, and remove the blue gap from the bottom lane's run-up area.
4. Teach air shifting in w2 (w2-04: a second E glyph over the yellow bridge's start, and a bigger take-off window).
5. Respawn with a key held: after a fall the runner respawns at once and keeps running (right is still held), so one mistake becomes several deaths and you cannot read what went wrong. A 0.5 s freeze or "release and press again" would help, in particular for the two-lane levels.

## Anything broken

- Nothing that looks like a hard bug in the build. Several observations that may be bugs or may be design:
  - In w2-02 (Coast) I reached the exit by pressing E three times at the spawn and jumping off the start platform at gear 3, landing on the yellow bridge and never touching the red one. Probably not intended; it skips the red bridge.
  - In my test at w2-01/w2-02, E presses within about 1.5 s after a death seemed to be ignored (the next life stayed at gear 1). I could not reproduce it deterministically, so I treat it as unverified.
  - Level timer: it counts from the level's load/last restart, not from the first input, and runs through falls and reading time. A time like 7:55.63 on a clear card looks silly to a voter. Best time on that clear card also records it.
  - The runner's HUD home glyph (BUG-8) is shown in the bottom strip for the bottom runner in w3-02/03/04 when it reaches its exit; I did not see the missing case in the top runner, so I did not re-test it.
  - The restart card overlaps the bottom lane.

## Scores for this slice (1-5)

- Overall 3. The shift-and-jump systems are inventive and the solving moments are good, but the last level is a wall.
- Gameplay 3.5. Great when it clicks, rough when two precise jumps and one rule are all hidden.
- Graphics 4. Clear pixel look; the color + pattern pairing reads well and the death/respawn feedback is clear.
- Audio: not rated, no audio in this setup.
- Innovation 4. Speed as the color, two lanes with their own gears.
- Theme 4. "Shift" is literal and used in every level.
