# Playtest M1-28: grey-box w0-02 with HUD, plus w1-01 to w1-05 (2026-11-04 build, filed as 2026-11-07)

Fresh-eyes rule followed. I read only `CLAUDE.md`, the M1-28 board line and design-lead's questions in `requests.md` before playing. I read my previous report (`2026-11-02-greybox.md`) only at the end. I did not read the GDD, specs, `levels/**`, `docs/design/**`, or any solution.

Screenshots are in `C:\dev\itch gam\docs\playtests\2026-11-07-m1-greybox\` (8 files, listed at the end).

## Method caveats (read these before trusting my numbers)
- I drove headless Chromium (Playwright, 960x540, ANGLE/D3D11, a steady 60 fps) through `http://localhost:5191/?level=<id>`. I am a script, not a human, so "attempts" below are my runs, and "human estimate" lines are my guess at what a voter would need.
- Headless software-GL ran at about 19 fps and made timing meaningless, so I switched to the D3D11 backend, which gave 60 fps.
- Same deviation as last time: I triggered key presses off `window.__redshift.state` (x position) instead of wall-clock waits, and I read the state trace (x, vx, gear, band, grounded) to see *why* I died. The route ideas came from screenshots and trial and error, but where a trace taught me a number (platform length, speed ramp) I say so.
- I cannot judge audio (headless, no sound), nor real-time reflex difficulty.
- Scratchpad note for the orchestrator: the shared scratchpad already held many files from other agents (including files named like solutions and "cheese" probes). I did not open them. I did overwrite three generic names there (`play.cjs`, `run.sh`, `montage.py`), so if another agent relied on those, they need to be regenerated.
- The build has no title screen or menus yet. `/` loads w0-02 directly, with a "Windows tip: tapping Shift 5 times opens Sticky Keys" card across the bottom third of the screen until Enter is pressed (screenshot 01). There was no level select, so I could not test the M2-30 menu questions.
- Port 5191 server is stopped. I did not touch tests, code or port 4173.

## What changed since my 2026-11-02 report
- There is now a HUD gear meter (my number one ask last time). It works; see the questions section.
- The jump is now a real platformer jump (3 tiles up), not a half-tile hop.
- The win banner now shows a time ("Level complete - 0:05.38 - press R"), not a meaningless step count.
- The five world-1 levels are a much smoother ramp than w0-02 was as an opener.

## First 30 seconds, from the screen alone
- On `/`, I see a white box on a grey platform, a dark card across the lower third telling me about Sticky Keys, and a gear meter in the top left (white square, then blue/green/yellow/red patterned icons labelled 1 to 4, with Q and E key caps on either side). Nothing says what to do. The Sticky Keys card hides a third of the level, including the lower platform and the red bridge.
- I press Right. The box turns blue with horizontal lines, the background tints navy and the dashed blue bridge fills in and goes solid. The HUD highlights "1". That reads well and is still the best moment in the game.
- I do not know where the exit is. In w0-02 it is not on the first screen at all (the camera scrolls later, and the level is about 52 tiles wide). In the w1 levels the whole level fits on one screen and the exit is a small grey square at the far right. It looks like a stray block, not a goal.

## Per level

### w0-02 Grey Box (the grey-box room)
- **Understood the new idea?** Rule one (moving = coloured, stopping = grey, coloured tiles only solid for matching colour) clicked in about 20 seconds. The full room I only solved with help from the state trace, over 14 runs. I believed the rule was "pick the gear whose colour matches the tile". The deeper rule I had to find by trial: **speed ramps up over distance, so gear 4 needs a run-up**, and that **the air drops your band one step on a long jump** (red takeoff, yellow landing).
- **Attempts and time:** about 14 runs, around 25 minutes of wall clock including experiments. Human estimate: 15 to 25 attempts, 10 to 20 minutes. A jam voter would quit well before the end.
- **Confusion and frustration:**
  1. First death: I walked off the end of the blue bridge and fell, then fell through the red strip below as blue. Nothing said "this is not the way" or "you are the wrong colour". The fall to the death line takes about a second and a half, so the player disappears down the screen with no cause shown.
  2. A mid-bridge release drops you through the blue bridge (you turn white at speed 0). Learnable, but the first time is a death with no hint.
  3. Shifting up while on the bridge dropped me through it (green on a blue bridge). I did this twice by accident, which says the "gear is your color right now" idea is not intuitive.
  4. Red needs run-up space the lower platform does not give from where you land (about 4 tiles of ramp; red needs about 5). The trace showed the solution is to walk back left to the wall, shift to 4, and run. The wall on the left helps, but nothing points at it. This is the same finding as last time.
  5. The yellow gap: I ran off the platform edge at red and yellow and fell short or flew past. The working answer is a jump as **red** at the platform edge so the air drag lowers you to yellow just in time to land on the yellow bridge. Jumping as yellow lowers me to green and I fell through; jumping with a short tap did nothing. That is the one place where "no air control + drag step" feels like a trap, not a puzzle, because the only feedback is a death.
  6. The end: you stand at the end of the blue bridge on a grey tile, the exit is visible in a dark pocket just below, and there is no path. The answer is to walk back onto the bridge tiles, then stop, so you turn grey and fall through into the pocket. It is a lovely idea and a very hard thing to notice (screenshot 03).
- **Difficulty:** 4 / 5. It is fair once every rule is known (every death traced to my own choice) but it stacks six ideas. As an opener it is far too hard; as the final exam of world 0 it works.
- **Fun:** high once solved. The run from the left wall across the red bridge at full speed with the screen tinted red (screenshot 02) is the best feeling in the build.
- **Cheese:** none new. Last time the red jump flew over the yellow bridge; this time the red jump from the platform edge lands on the yellow bridge and then runs on, so the bridge is used as designed (the drag step is what makes it work). I did not find a wall clip.

### w1-01 First Steps
- **Understood?** Yes, instantly. There is nothing else to try: hold Right, bridges turn solid under you.
- **Attempts and time:** 1 attempt, 7.8 seconds to the banner.
- **Confusion:** (1) Nothing tells me to hold Right, but no other action looks useful. (2) The exit is a small, low-contrast grey square, not obviously a goal. (3) When I jump on the bridge, the roof leaves about half a tile of headroom, so I get a small bonk-hop instead of a jump. That reads as "low ceiling" but also as "the game won't let me jump here".
- **Difficulty:** 1 / 5, fair. It is a tutorial, and a decent one.
- **Fun:** low but fine (it is meant to be a 10 second warm-up).
- **Cheese:** none.

### w1-02 Shift
- **Understood?** After 2 tries. I held Right, turned blue, and fell through the green bridge (screenshot 08). The HUD has a green dotted slot with a "2" and an E key cap, so the answer is visible, and I tried E on the second go. I thought the rule was "be the colour of the bridge".
- **Attempts and time:** 3 runs. First: hold Right, die. Second: E then Right, crossed the green bridge, forgot to go back to blue and fell. Third: E, Right, then Q on the middle platform, won. Human estimate: 3 to 5 attempts, about 40 seconds.
- **Frustration points:** the middle grey platform is only about two tiles wide, and I have to drop from green (6 tiles/s) to blue in that space. My one press at x=8.4 worked with about a tile to spare; I did not map the full window. A human who presses Q late falls through the blue bridge with no warning. That is the first "I did it right but too slowly" death.
- **Difficulty:** 2 / 5, fair. The punishment for a late Q is a fast respawn.
- **Fun:** it is where the shift verb first feels like a verb.
- **Cheese:** none.

### w1-03 Runway
- **Understood?** After 3 tries. I thought the rule was "get to red, which is gear 4". Hold Right (blue) fell through the red bridge. Then E four times from the start position and Right: the speed only reached yellow when I hit the bridge and I fell. The aha is that the start position is in the middle of the platform and the platform keeps going left behind you; the thin pillar on the left edge is the cue. Walk left to the wall, shift to 4, run (screenshot 06). The trace shows red speed needs about 5 tiles of ramp.
- **Attempts and time:** 3 runs. Winning run was 5.38 seconds. Human estimate: 4 to 6 attempts, one minute.
- **Frustration points:** four quick E taps in my script produced only gear 3 when pressed with no gap (they merged), and a human mashing E might see the same. The failed runs were not obvious: you shift to 4 and still lose, because gear 4 is the intent but the speed is not there yet. The HUD shows slot "4" lit with the player still yellow and no cue that speed is still building (the speed-vs-gear distinction is subtle; see questions).
- **Difficulty:** 3 / 5. The idea is clear once you think "I need room". Fair.
- **Fun:** good. The name "Runway" plus the pillar at the far left makes the answer discoverable.
- **Cheese:** none found.

### w1-04 Brake
- **Understood?** After 2 tries. The naive plan (E four times, run) clears the red bridge and then runs the player at red speed over the blue bridge and into the pit. Then it is clear: slow down on the middle platform.
- **Attempts and time:** 2 runs. Q pressed three times as I left the red bridge (x=13.2) worked first time. Human estimate: 3 to 4 attempts, 45 seconds.
- **Frustration points:** the middle platform is about 6 tiles, and braking from red to blue (12 to 3 tiles/s) uses roughly 4 of them. So the margin for pressing Q late is about 1.5 tiles, a fifth of a second at red speed. Pressing Q while still on the red bridge would drop you through it (I did not test this). The exit is off screen to the right at the start, so the player cannot see how long the blue bridge is.
- **Difficulty:** 3 / 5. Fair, with a tight window.
- **Fun:** good. The red-then-blue snap makes "gears are a tool" land.
- **Cheese:** none found.

### w1-05 Red Wall
- **Understood?** After about 3 tries. I thought the rule was "red goes on the red bridge", so I shifted to 4 and ran straight into the red wall at the end of the platform: I stop dead (band 0) and the thud reads as a bug at first, because the wall is the same colour as the bridge I need. Then I tried running at gear 3, which passes through the wall (it is not solid for yellow), and shifted up **after** the wall and before the bridge. The aha is that the red wall is only solid to red.
- **Attempts and time:** about 5 runs while measuring. Human estimate: 4 to 6 attempts, one minute and a half.
- **Frustration points:** the window is a single grey tile between the wall and the bridge, and speed ramps from 9 to 12 in about 0.1 s. In my tests, pressing E anywhere from x=8.4 (before the wall) to x=10.5 worked and x=10.9 failed, so in practice it is a window of about two tiles, about 0.2 s. Pressing E too early does not bounce me off the wall because the wall only blocks once the band has reached red (the ramp finished after the wall). That is forgiving, but it means the "pass below red then shift up" logic is not what the screen shows.
- **Difficulty:** 3.5 / 5. Fair but the best late-game puzzle in the set. Not a first-world level for a casual voter.
- **Fun:** very good. It is the first level where I felt clever.
- **Cheese:** pressing E early (before the wall) also works because of how the speed ramp meets the wall. I did not find a way to skip the wall completely.

## Answers to design-lead's M1-28 questions
1. **Before the red bridge, can I say which gear red needs?** Yes, from the HUD alone: the red crosshatch icon is slot 4, so "four notches up". That is a big improvement over last time. What I could not tell from the HUD was how much room gear 4 needs. In w1-03 I pressed E to slot 4 and still failed because the speed had not built up. A voter will read "4 is lit, why am I yellow?" as a bug.
2. **Did the first jump look like a full jump?** Yes (screenshot 07). It rises 3 tiles, about 1.5 times the player's height, and the arc is easy to read. Different from the half-tile hop last time. The exception is under the world-1 bridge roofs, where there is no room to jump (see M2-30 extra).
3. **Could I spot the exit and the yellow ghosts at a glance?** Yellow ghosts: yes, the dashed yellow outline with chevrons stands out against the dark background and the pattern means it is readable without colour. The exit: no. It is a small grey square the same tone as the walls, and in w0-02 it is not on the first screen at all. In the w1 levels it was only findable because the whole level fits on one screen. In w0-02 the exit is dark and sits in a pocket (screenshot 03).
4. **Did I notice the drag meter and the pending-shift bracket?** I noticed a thin bar under the gear slots while I was shifting and while airborne (screenshot 05, top frame). I had no idea what it meant; it reads as a loading bar. I did not catch a pending-shift bracket in any frame. I do not think a voter will notice either without a one-line hint.
5. **Do the world-1 roofs feel like part of the room or like a block put there to stop you?** I did not try to jump over a bridge until I deliberately tested it. In w1-01 and w1-02 the roofs are two separate grey pillars hanging above the bridges, with a gap of sky between them and no connection to anything. They read as scenery, and I never felt "blocked" while walking. When I jumped on a bridge I got a half-tile hop, which felt like a low tunnel ceiling, not an arbitrary block. They would feel better as a part of a room (a ceiling run that links the two pillars) than as hanging slabs.
6. **M2-30 extras (worlds 2 and 3 open, w3-01 before finishing world 1, starting a world cold from hints, menus firing by themselves after a level ends):** not testable. This build has no title, level select, hint glyphs or results screen. After a win the banner shows "Level complete - ... - press R" with a second line "Enter: next level"; two keys named, one for restart and one for next, with the restart one the most prominent (screenshot 06).

## Overall
- **Would I finish it as a jam voter?** I would finish w1-01 to w1-05 in about 4 to 5 minutes. I would not finish w0-02 if it is what `/` loads. I would quit at the red bridge in w0-02 after the third death, or before that if I read the Sticky Keys card as an error.
- **Rough cheese note:** none dangerous.

## Scores (1 to 5)
- **Overall: 3.** The world-1 ramp is nice and clear; the entry point (w0-02) and the exit marker hold it back.
- **Gameplay: 3.5.** The shift/brake/run-up puzzles are tight and each of the five levels teaches one thing. Some timing windows are very small.
- **Graphics: 3.** Clean, readable and the HUD is a clear step up. Still plain. The exit and the roofs read badly.
- **Audio: n/a.** I could not judge it.
- **Innovation: 4.** One axis (speed) giving colour, solidity and gear in one is a fresh use of "shift".
- **Theme (SHIFT): 4.** Gear shifts, colour shifts and the air-drag step all fit; the HUD makes "shift" the verb.

## Top 5 fixes, ranked
1. **Do not open on w0-02.** `/` loads w0-02, covers a third of it with the Sticky Keys card, hides the exit, and asks six ideas at once. Load w1-01 first and make w0-02 a late-world-0 exam. This is the single biggest quit risk.
2. **Make the exit unmistakable.** A glowing or animated doorway, distinct from wall tiles and visible at a glance, and a first-screen hint (arrow or small pan) that the level extends past the screen in w0-02 and w1-04. In w0-02 the final pocket also needs a visual cue that it is reachable by dropping through.
3. **Show why you fell.** On the first mismatched-colour fall, flash the tile outline and pulse the matching HUD slot (for example "2" glows when green is needed). Right now the player slides out of the screen for about a second and a half with no cause shown.
4. **Show speed building, not just the selected gear.** In w1-03 and w0-02 slot 4 is lit while you are still yellow. Make the "current band" marker (the big square at slot 0) track the real band more visibly, and give the thin bar under the slots a label or a short first-time hint (it is the drag meter; I took it for a loading bar). Also hint once that you can shift in the air.
5. **Widen or telegraph the tight windows.** w1-02 middle platform (about 2 tiles for green to blue), w1-04 (about 1.5 tiles of slack after the red bridge) and w1-05 (about 0.2 s). For a voter with a real keyboard these are one miss each, but each miss looks like a bug until the rule is clear. Also fix the end card: "press R" and "Enter: next level" fight each other; make next the primary action.
