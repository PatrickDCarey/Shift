## Possibly broken
- Taps of RS/LS closer than about 5 frames apart sometimes lose one shift (w4-04: three LS taps 2-3 frames apart ended at gear 2 instead of gear 1).
- At w4-02 the gear picker from standing white: two RS taps put the HUD on gear 3 (white start counts as gear 0 and the first tap goes to 2?) - confusing when stopped on a shuttle.
- w4-03 / w4-05 moving bridge: HUD says gear 2 while standing (selected), but pace is white until you run; nothing tells the player that the selected gear is only a ceiling and that pace must be earned over distance. Cost me 2 deaths (w4-03) and 1 (w4-05).

## Top five changes (as seen)
1. Tell the player that a gear is a pace you must run up to (w4-03/w4-05 bridges, w2-04 air-lock): a one-line hint or a visible "pace" fill on the HUD.
2. Shuttle/lift dwell time at the docks that matter (post-side ferry in w4-05): lengthen the dwell or let it wait while the runner is within a tile.
3. w2-04: air gear lock is never taught before this level; the shift+jump same-frame requirement is nearly a trick. Teach lock in w2-01.
4. Restart cost in w4-05: add a post before the moving-bridge room (I saw no post before room 4; first post only after the blue bridge).
5. w4-04: the front forces slow blue pace on a long bridge; consider a shorter blue bridge or a slower front.
