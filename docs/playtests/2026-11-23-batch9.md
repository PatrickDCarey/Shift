### How I counted and jitter
- Keys are scripted through Playwright with 80-100 ms taps and fixed `sleep()` waits. The game ran at about 55 fps in headless Chromium.
- Page screenshots taken mid-run stall the sim to about 4 fps, which shifted my timings. I therefore avoided mid-run screenshots after the early experiments.
- Timing jitter is roughly +/-100 ms, about 20 px of runner travel. That is enough to wreck any section with a narrow timing window.
- A "life" is one death and restart. On w3-05 I counted about 11. They were varied blind attempts: shift up once, twice or three times, jump at several times, and a back-up-left test.

### w3-05 what I saw
- Top lane: a long flat grey runway, a blue bridge over a pit at about x=544-640, a ceiling block above it, and the exit door far right.
- Bottom lane: a short platform (x 30-256), a red bridge (256-416), a pit, a yellow bridge (640-768), then a platform.
- I did not see any step in the start screen. The only thing at the back of the run-up is a left wall.
- Holding D at gear 1 reaches the end of the bottom platform at about 3.5 s, still blue. The bottom runner drops through the red bridge and the level restarts after about 3 s.
- Shifting up once, twice or three times before the platform end did not get the band past blue or green by x=256. It needs distance, and there is only about 220 px.
- The run-up is too short to build the band the red bridge asks for, so the player has no idea what to try. I could not tell whether to jump, brake, back up or wait. This is the same "what is the verb here" problem the batch 8 notes were fixing.

## Per level

### w3-01 "Two Lanes"
- **Started from the level select**, not Play. Play points at 1-1, and world 3 level 1 is open from a fresh save. I did not play worlds 1-2.
- **Understood the new idea?** After about 8 tries. My rule: both runners move on one input; each lane has its own colored bridges.
- **Attempts and time:** about 9 lives, and most of the time went on learning controls and my own tooling.
  - Shift keys: I found that E and X both shift up and Q/Z shift down only from the Controls screen.
  - A shift made while moving changes the band within about 250 ms, but a shift made at a standstill just changes the gear marker.
- **Confusion:**
  - The glyph panel shows "R-shift / E X" but not which of them is up and which is down, and the HUD does not say which slot is "selected" and which is "current".
  - The runner is white when still, so I did not understand that white means "nothing colored is solid".
  - The restart card appears on death.
- **Cheese:** none. **Difficulty 2/5. Fun 3/5.**

### w3-02 "Out of Step"
- **Understood?** Late, after about 20 attempts. The rule I found: the top runner is stopped by a low block while the bottom keeps running, so they get out of step.
- **Attempts and time:** about 20 lives, roughly 25 minutes (a lot lost to my timing and to the driver crashing twice).
- **Solution I found:**
  1. Run to the platform, shift up on the grey platform (the window is only about 150 ms wide), and let go while the bottom runner is mid-platform.
  2. Tap Left for about 0.4 s so both back up. The top gets a run-up and the bottom stays on grey.
  3. Run, jump the block, and cross both green bridges.
  4. The clear card said 11.8 s once I had the sequence.
- **Confusion:**
  - A standing jump against the block does nothing, with no feedback.
  - Backing up onto the other lane's bridge kills the bottom runner. This is the same kind of back-up the w3-05 notes ask for, and it took me a long time to find.
  - A white runner standing on a colored tile falls through it, and nothing explains that.
- **Cheese:** none. **Difficulty 4/5, borderline unfair because of the narrow shift window. Fun 3/5.**
- Evidence: `evidence/b9-w302-solve.png`, `evidence/b9-w302-clear.png`.

## Answers to the first 30 seconds
- The title screen and level select are clear and fast. The first w3-01 screen showed a Windows Sticky Keys tip, which I dismissed with Enter. The glyph hints in the level (move, shift up, jump) are readable.
- What I did not understand without reading: that gear is a speed cap rather than an instant band, that the HUD slot with a bracket is the selected gear while the filled slot is the current band, and that a white runner is "colorless" and falls through every colored bridge.

## Top five changes
1. **Make the shift window forgiving on w3-02 and w3-05.** The window to shift on a grey platform is about 150 ms and about 20 px. Scripted or not, that is too narrow. Widen the platform or slow the band change.
2. **Show the gear vs band distinction in the HUD** (selected gear marker vs current band) and say once what white means.
3. **Explain "back up".** The back-up verb is the key idea of w3-02 and w3-05 and nothing on screen offers it. A hint glyph on the left wall, or a short back-up run in w3-01 as a free lesson, would do.
4. **Give feedback for a failed standing jump** and for being held at a kerb (a small bump or glyph), so the player learns "I am held" rather than "nothing happens".
5. **Shorten the death loop**: about 3 s from fall to a playable restart is long for a level that takes 10-20 attempts; the auto restart also swallowed my early R presses.

## Possibly broken
- **Restart timing:** after a death the level auto-restarts after about 3 s. Pressing R inside that window sometimes gave a restart that ignored the next held key. I could not tell whether that is a bug or my timing.
- **Standing jump** against a 1-tile block does nothing, with no visual response. It may be intended, but it reads as a dropped input.
- **Playwright crashed the harness twice** (the Node driver died mid-session, not the game). I lost save state once and had to replay w3-01.
