# Trailer shot list (30–45 s)

_Owner: design-lead. Written 2026-11-24 (B12-A3) for the 23-level build. The owner cuts the trailer and makes the new page artwork after this phase with their own tools; this is the design brief, not an edit plan. Page copy: `itch-page.md`._

## What it must say

The theme is **SHIFT**: **your pace sets your color, and only your color is solid.** A viewer with the sound off should understand that rule in the first 10 seconds (pillar 2), then see it reach things that move (slabs, bolts) and see that long levels have checkpoints. No claim the build does not make: no sweep footage unless w4-04/w5-04 ship, no leaderboard footage unless leaderboards are live.

## Capture

- From the packaged build, 960×540 or fullscreen at 1920×1080 (4×, pixel-sharp). HUD visible (the gear meter is the second way the color reads). No debug overlays.
- Clean takes: the dev/QA build's replay URL (`?level=<id>&replay=1&speed=1`) plays each level's recording; record your own runs for the fails.
- Fails are good footage: a fall-through is the rule in one shot.
- Text: only a key-cap overlay where noted ("SHIFT") and the end card. Music: the game's adaptive track follows pace, so cut to it; use audio only after the owner's own listening check (`itch-page.md` §3 note).

## Shots

| # | Time | Level and moment | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–4 s | **w1-01 First Steps:** walk blue across the blue bridge, let go mid-bridge, turn white, drop through. | The rule in one beat: stop and the floor is gone. |
| 2 | 4–9 s | **w1-03 Runway:** back to the wall, sprint; the runner goes white, blue, green, yellow, red, gear meter climbing, and crosses the red bridge. Overlay a "SHIFT" key cap on the presses. | Pace sets color; speed takes distance. The theme word on screen. |
| 3 | 9–13 s | **w1-05 Red Wall:** arrive yellow, pass through the red wall, shift up, cross the red bridge red. | A wall stops only its own color. |
| 4 | 13–17 s | **w2-02 Coast:** red takeoff, the drag ring at 6 tiles, red to yellow, land on the yellow pad. | Commit at takeoff; air costs speed. |
| 5 | 17–20 s | **w3-03 Gear Split:** two runners, one stopped blue under the block, the twin flying yellow. | One set of keys, two colors (depth). Short. |
| 6 | 20–25 s | **w4-03 Moving Bridge:** a white runner drops through the green slab (a fail), then a green runner crosses it while it rests level. Or w4-01: standing on the grey lift and staying white. | New: platforms move, and colored ones still obey the rule. |
| 7 | 25–31 s | **w5-01 Spark:** a red bolt passes through the walking blue runner; cut to **w5-03 Hurdles:** a green jump over a grey foot-height bolt. | New: bolts hit only their own color; grey hits everyone. Dodge by color first, jump second. |
| 8 | 31–36 s | **w5-05 Full Spectrum (or w4-05 Commute):** the runner passes a post and it lights; a death in the next room; respawn at the post. | Long levels, and you keep your progress. |
| 9 | 36–42 s | **w5-05 montage, fastest beats:** the red Coast through the yellow fountain (red, so it passes harmlessly), crossing the rising lift under white rain while walking, the blue walk home to the exit. | The finale combines everything; ends on a win. |
| 10 | 42–45 s | **End card:** REDSHIFT in the game's pixel font, tagline "Your speed is your color. Only your color is solid.", "GitHub Game Off 2026", "Play free on itch.io". | |

**If it must be 30 s:** drop 5 and 9, and keep 1–4, 6–8 and 10.

## Five-line summary

1. w1-01: stop on a blue bridge, turn white, fall: the rule.
2. w1-03 and w1-05: sprint white to red with a SHIFT key cap; pass a red wall in yellow.
3. w4-03: a green slab drops a white runner and holds a green one crossing at pace.
4. w5-01 and w5-03: a red bolt passes through blue; a green jump over a grey bolt.
5. w5-05: a post lights, death, respawn at the post; finale montage, end card.
