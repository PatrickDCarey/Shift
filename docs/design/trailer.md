# Trailer: a teaser (about 28 s)

_Owner: design-lead. Written 2026-11-24 (B12-A3) as a 30–45 s guide-style brief. **Rewritten 2026-11-25** after the owner watched the first cut (36 s) and said: "I'm worried about the trailer being too much of a guide." It is now a teaser: two captions (the pitch), fails and action beats, glimpses of each world, no level's answer. British spelling (`decisions.md` 2026-11-25). Page copy: `itch-page.md`._

## What it must say

Two lines, and nothing else in words: **YOUR SPEED IS YOUR COLOUR** and **ONLY YOUR COLOUR IS SOLID.** A viewer with the sound off should get that rule in the first 10 seconds (pillar 2). After that the trailer promises, without explaining: things move, things fly at you, runs get long, and the rule still holds. The viewer should leave knowing *what* the game asks, never *how* any room is solved.

No claim the build does not make: no leaderboard footage unless leaderboards are live; the sweep (w4-04, w5-04) only while both are in the build.

## The spoiler rule

**May be shown:**
- **The base rule, in full, from w1-01 to w1-03** (First Steps, Shift, Runway): a fall-through, a sprint from white to red, a red crossing. These rooms *are* the pitch, and the GIF on the page already shows w1-03.
- **A glimpse of each new thing**, at most about 2 s, cut **before the outcome** unless the outcome is a death: a wall bump, a drag ring mid-flight, two runners on one set of keys, a slab moving, a bolt passing through a runner, a hurdle jump at its apex, the sweep's front behind a runner, a post lighting.
- **Deaths, anywhere.** A fall-through, a bolt hit or a respawn shows a rule biting, not the answer. (Not a death that is one input away from the answer and framed so the answer shows, e.g. w1-05's red bump followed by the same runner going through blue.)
- **Section 1 of w5-05** (up to post 1) as action, never a whole room from approach to safe landing.

**May not be shown:**
- **A complete solution to any level after w1-03:** never the approach, the deciding input and the safe arrival in one continuous take or in consecutive shots of the same room. Concretely: no w1-05 shift-up past the red wall, no w2-02 landing on the yellow pad, no w4-03 crossing on the slab, no w5-02 moving brake, no Tandem split resolving into both bodies home.
- **The finale's last rooms:** nothing from w5-05 after post 2 (Hurdles, Stay down, Brake, Runway, short Coast, Home), and **never an exit touch or a results card** after w1-03.
- **Key-cap overlays on any level after w1-03** (a key cap at a place is a hint for that place).
- **Captions other than the two rule lines.** No feature captions (PLATFORMS MOVE, BOLTS HIT THEIR OWN COLOUR, CHECKPOINTS are cut): the pictures say it, and a caption per feature turns the teaser into a manual.

**The ending:** on an action beat cut to black (a runner in mid-air), then the end card. Not on a win, not on the finale's exit.

## Capture

- From the packaged build, 960×540 or fullscreen at 1920×1080 (4×, pixel-sharp). HUD visible (the gear meter is the second way the colour reads). No debug overlays.
- Clean takes: the dev/QA build's replay URL (`?level=<id>&replay=1&speed=1`) for the rule shots; record your own runs for the fails and glimpses.
- **Re-capture the bolt shots (8, 9, 12) after the bolt-visibility art lands** (`decisions.md` 2026-11-25 world-5 triage): today's grey and white bolts read as sparkles in a still and worse in a fast cut.
- Captions in the game's pixel font, white with a dark outline so they read on every world tint, upper third, one line each. Music: cut to the game's adaptive track only after the owner's own listening check (`itch-page.md` §3 note); otherwise any CC0 track listed in `CREDITS.md`.
