## Watch list (not top risks)
- **Windows Sticky Keys** interrupts play after five Shift presses. Mitigated and a **Must**: Q/E and Z/X bindings (built, M1-16), the one-time tip (view built, mounted in M2-39, rebuilt at 480×270 in PX-9). Checked with an emulated Windows user agent in M2-28. Owner: ui-engineer.
- **Pixel judder and legibility.** At 60 fps the world moves in uneven whole-pixel steps (ADR). Checked in PX-11 and the M2 playtest; the deferred remedy (482×272 canvas with a CSS translate) is about 4 h and is not built unless asked. Owner: tech-lead.
- **Color is never the only signal at 12×24 px.** Dots pattern has the least margin (16.7% against a 15% rule); red crosshatch can read as a grate. Checked in PX-1 and M2-14b. Owner: art-director.
- **Determinism drift.** Core-purity arch test and golden hash exist; Tandem adds a second body, so T-12 adds a two-body golden hash. Owner: tech-lead.
- **Control model churn.** The model changed once (v1.0 to v1.1) and the twist changed today. The control model and tuning are frozen at M2-31; anything after is a user decision with a cost note from the producer. Owner: design-lead.
- **Windows line endings.** `.gitattributes`, CRLF-tolerant parser and codec, and a CRLF test exist. Owner: tech-lead.
- **Public flip is a single point of failure for jam eligibility.** Repo private until USER-6 on Nov 30. Secrets audit M4-09, reminder at the M4 gate, S-02 checks logged out. Dec 1 buffer. Owner: producer.
- **Release bundle size and spoilers.** The scene bundles `levels/*.solution.txt` for replay mode; tech-lead decides at M1-33 whether they ship. Bundle was 1.25 MB (337 KB gzip) on 2026-11-03. Owner: tech-lead.
- **Browser audio autoplay and iframe focus** on itch.io: M1-24, M2-39, embed check M4-15. Owner: audio-designer and ui-engineer.
